Reading Pretzel Hand

The letters

Each box is one throw and the catch that comes after it, made by the same hand. The hands take turns, starting with the right hand. The coloured line under a box shows which hand: right, left.

R rArms crossed, right arm on top.
L lArms crossed, left arm on top.
U uArms uncrossed.

A capital letter is the throw; a small letter is the catch. Crossed means both hands are over on the other side of the body. If it's the other arm that's on top, this hand is underneath: an under-the-arm throw or catch.

Between the boxes

:Change side: the next throws are made on the other side of the body.
–Change of direction on the same side (outside to inside, or back), as in the butterfly messes.
.A held 2: the ball stays in the hand rather than being thrown.
U(u)A 1: the ball goes straight across to the other hand, which catches it (the letter in brackets). The throw just before it has no catch letter of its own.

Before the letters

423The siteswap: how long each throw stays in the air, throw by throw. With no number the pattern is all 3s. A single number, like 4, is the number of balls. A T after a 2 means that 2 is thrown, not held.
oOutside throws: from the side of the pattern in to the middle.
iInside throws: from the middle out to the side.
OOutside to outside: right across the pattern.
IColumns: straight up, caught where it was thrown.
iooSeveral direction letters take turns, throw by throw. Each throw's own direction is shown after its number, above the letters.
LJust before the slash: the pattern starts with the left hand.
\ /Where the first throw peaks: the top of the slash points to the side of the body the first ball peaks on, as you look at the juggler. / peaks on the juggler's left (your right) and \ on the juggler's right (your left). It's a guide, not a change to the pattern. Sebs is the exception: it has the mills letters with the opposite slash, and is juggled at the sides. Columns go straight up, and O throws peak in the middle, so their slash doesn't point anywhere.

"TR" in a name means time reverse: the pattern you'd see if you filmed another one and played it backwards.
